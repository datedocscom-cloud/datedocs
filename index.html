The process
Intentional from the very first step

No swiping. No algorithms optimizing for engagement. Just a thoughtful, human-centered process designed for people whose time is precious.

01
🔐

Credential verification

Before your profile is visible, our team verifies your medical license through official state boards. Your peers deserve to know who you truly are.

02
💬

Intent interview

A written questionnaire — and optionally a brief video call with a Lumina advisor — establishes that your intentions toward marriage and lasting partnership are sincere.

03
🌿

Values-first matching

We surface a curated handful of compatible profiles weekly — weighted by life goals, family intentions, spirituality, and lifestyle — not just attraction.

04
💌

Thoughtful introductions

When mutual interest exists, Lumina facilitates a warm introduction with conversation prompts tailored to your shared values — not a cold message into the void.

05
🤝

Counselor support

Optional access to relationship counselors who deeply understand the physician lifestyle — the long hours, the emotional weight, and the extraordinary rewards.

06
💍

A life together

Our goal is not engagement metrics. Our goal is for you to delete this app because you found your person. That's the only success we celebrate.

Our commitments
Integrity is not a feature — it is the foundation

Physicians are uniquely vulnerable to those who seek proximity to status or income. Lumina exists specifically to protect against that — and to attract partners who are drawn to you.

✓
Medical license verification
Every member's credentials are confirmed through official state licensing boards before any profile is published. No exceptions.
🛡
Genuine intent screening
Non-physician applicants complete an extensive questionnaire reviewed by human advisors. Those seeking status or financial proximity are declined.
⚖
No gamification or dark patterns
Lumina does not optimize for time-on-app. We have no streak rewards, no push notifications engineered for compulsion. Your wellbeing comes first.
🚫
Zero tolerance for misrepresentation
Any member who misrepresents their identity, profession, financial situation, or intentions is immediately and permanently removed.
🔒
Private by default
Your profile is never searchable by name. Photos are blurred until mutual interest is established. Your privacy is protected at every stage.

"You have given years to your patients. You deserve a partner who chooses you for who you are — fully, freely, and sincerely."

◆Every non-physician member is screened by a human advisor before approval
◆Compatibility weighting prioritizes shared values and life goals over appearance alone
◆Profiles are introduced slowly and intentionally — maximum 5 new profiles per week
◆Relationship counselors available throughout your journey, not just at crisis moments
◆We celebrate relationships that lead to deletion. That's the point.
